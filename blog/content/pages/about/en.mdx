tipmonk is where [Inog Kim](/en/author/inog-kim/), a strategic-investment practitioner, writes up what they study. The name comes from Tip, a little chipmunk monk who collects useful tips.

## What it covers

- **Concepts**: ideas and deal terms strategic investors use, from SI vs FI and corporate venture capital to RCPS and shareholder-agreement clauses
- **Reading filings**: walk-throughs of Korean (DART) and US (SEC) disclosures
- **Deal analysis**: real, public deals broken down from their filings
- **Event studies**: for listed companies, how the stock moved around the announcement compared with the market

## Principles

1. **Public information only.** Every analysis rests on filings and public sources, linked in the post. Deals or companies the author has worked on, or may hold non-public information about, are off limits.
2. **No recommendations.** Nothing here recommends buying or selling a security or sets a price target. Event studies look back at prices that have already moved.
3. **Corrections.** Spot an error? Tell us through the [contact page](/en/contact/). We fix the post and note the update date.

## Event-study method

By default we use a market-adjusted model: a stock's daily return minus the same day's index return (KOSPI or KOSDAQ for Korea, typically the S&P 500 for the US) is the abnormal return, summed over a window around the announcement as the cumulative abnormal return (CAR). Announcements after the close count from the next trading day. The model is simple and transparent, but it doesn't adjust for a stock's beta or for other news in the window.
