**Inog Kim (인옥킴)** is the pen name of a practitioner who works in strategic investment at a company. The work is about inorganic growth, hence the name: *inorganic* plus *Kim*.

The posts write up concepts and deal terms studied on the job, follow real public deals through their DART and SEC filings, and, for listed companies, measure how the market reacted after the announcement.

## Ground rules

- Only public filings and sources are used; deals or companies the author has worked on are off limits.
- Posts are personal study notes and do not represent the views of the author's employer.
- Nothing here is investment advice. See the [disclaimer](/en/disclaimer/).

Questions and corrections are welcome through the [contact page](/en/contact/).
