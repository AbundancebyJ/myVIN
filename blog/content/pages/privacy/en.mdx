## What we collect

tipmonk has no accounts or comments and does not collect personal information you type in. Our host, Cloudflare, processes technical data such as IP addresses and browser type to deliver and protect the site, and we may use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies, for visit statistics.

## Advertising

tipmonk may show Google AdSense ads. If it does, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on visits to this and other sites. You can turn off personalized ads in [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com) or at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/). We will update this policy, and show any consent notice required by law, before ads go live.
